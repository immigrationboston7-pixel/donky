# Oil Panic Multi Screen (iPhone)

A fan-made tribute to the 1982 Nintendo Game & Watch **Oil Panic** (OP-51) clamshell
LCD handheld, rebuilt as an iPhone web app. It's plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript
with no build step.

Play it at **https://immigrationboston7-pixel.github.io/donky/oil-panic/**. To install
it, open that link in Safari and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. It then runs full
screen and works offline.

## How to play

Oil drips from a leaky pipe at a gas station. Catch it in your bucket on the top
screen, then carry it out to a balcony and pour it down into the boss's drum on
the bottom screen.

| Control | Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Move | LEFT / RIGHT | Arrow keys (or A / D) |
| Start Game A (normal) / Game B (fast) | GAME A / GAME B | 1 / 2 |
| Clock (attract mode) | TIME | T |
| Sound on/off | SOUND | M |

* **Catch:** the bucket holds 3 drops, and each catch is worth 1 point.
* **Pour:** step out onto the left or right balcony to tip the bucket. The oil falls to the
  bottom screen. If the boss is under that balcony, his drum catches it and you score
  1 point for 1 drop, 2 for 2, or 5 for a full bucket. If he isn't there, a customer gets
  splashed and you get a miss.
* **Fire:** a drop that hits the floor, or a 4th drop caught in a full bucket, starts a fire. That's a miss.
* **300 points:** if you have no misses you get *Chance Time*, where the score flashes and points count double until your next miss. If you do have misses, they are cleared.
* **Misses:** the 4th miss ends the game. The game gets faster as your score climbs.
